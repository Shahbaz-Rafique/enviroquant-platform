# EnviroQuant Vision Documents

## Strategic Vision

- EnviroQuant_Strategic_Platform_Vision.md

This directory contains the strategic vision and long-term direction of the EnviroQuant platform.

The Strategic Platform Vision serves as the primary reference document for:

- Product development
- Platform architecture
- AI intelligence design
- Environmental methodology
- Roadmap planning
- Investor discussions
- Advisory board discussions
- Strategic partnerships

The vision establishes EnviroQuant as an:

**Environmental Intelligence Operating System™**

designed to evolve from Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) intelligence into a comprehensive environmental decision-support ecosystem covering:

- Environmental Intelligence
- Compliance Intelligence
- Decision Intelligence
- Technology Intelligence
- Carbon Intelligence
- ESG Intelligence
- Maritime & MARPOL Intelligence
- Environmental Monitoring
- Environmental Digital Twin™

All future modules, workflows, intelligence engines, and platform capabilities should align with the Strategic Platform Vision.
