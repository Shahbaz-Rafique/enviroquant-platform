# EnviroQuant MVP 1 boundaries

## Objective

MVP 1 delivers the consultant-side EIA workspace and the foundational reviewer/regulator workflow:

`Project → structured authoring → evidence mapping → deterministic compliance classification → human review → export`

## Included

- Multi-tenant authentication, role-based access, projects, EIA membership and assignments.
- Eight-section, versioned EIA checklist with section and subsection authoring.
- Rich text, autosave, progress, comments, attachments, revisions and workflow transitions.
- Versioned source documents, PDF/DOCX/text/spreadsheet extraction and image OCR.
- Previous-EIA mapping with semantic suggestions and explicit user confirmation.
- Versioned regulations and standards with requirement-to-checklist tags.
- Evidence-grounded AI extraction of findings, gaps, recommendations and uncertainty.
- Deterministic classifications: `COMPLIANT`, `PARTIALLY_COMPLIANT`, `NEEDS_IMPROVEMENT`, `MISSING_INFORMATION`, and `NEEDS_REVIEW`.
- Versioned evaluation runs, claim-level citations, comparisons, approval requests and audit events.
- EIA and review exports in JSON, DOCX and PDF.
- Durable database-backed evaluation jobs.

Numeric scores and A–E grades are disabled by default. They may only be enabled as an explicitly configured, deterministic reporting feature.

## Deferred

- Billing and subscription management.
- Live sensor, IoT and emissions-monitoring integrations.
- Automated publication of regulatory updates without human validation.
- Cross-tenant benchmarking datasets and environmental knowledge graphs.
- Large-scale multi-region orchestration.

## Success criteria

Authorized users can build or import an EIA, trace every finding to project evidence or a versioned regulation, receive transparent rule-based classifications, resolve reviewer feedback, approve a review run and generate submission-ready exports.

Every implementation prioritizes reliability, tenant isolation, traceability, reproducibility and human review authority.
