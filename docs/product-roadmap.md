# EnviroQuant product roadmap

## MVP 1 — Consultant EIA and compliance workspace

The current MVP combines the platform foundation, smart EIA builder and foundational review workflow.

Delivered capabilities:

- Authentication, RBAC, tenant isolation, project teams and document-level assignments.
- Structured EIA builder, reusable content search, evidence management and revision history.
- Previous-report parsing, semantic checklist mapping and non-destructive checklist synchronization.
- Versioned regulations, checklist, prompts, models, methodology and deterministic rules.
- AI-assisted evidence analysis with deterministic classifications and human review.
- Review queues, comments, approvals, comparisons and JSON/DOCX/PDF exports.
- Database-backed evaluation workers, automated tests and CI/CD validation.

## Phase 2 — Submission and regulator operations

- Submission packages and regulator intake queues.
- Requests for information, statutory deadlines and formal decision records.
- Jurisdiction-specific templates and controlled regulatory content publication.
- Organization reporting based on transparent status counts.

## Phase 3 — Environmental intelligence

- Validated cross-project and sector benchmarks.
- Regulatory change monitoring with expert approval.
- Environmental trend analysis and reusable knowledge graphs.
- External GIS, laboratory and monitoring-system integrations.

## Long-term principles

The platform remains evidence-grounded, deterministic at the decision layer, versioned, reproducible, auditable and subject to human authority. AI may extract and explain information; it must not silently create evidence or make an untraceable regulatory decision.
