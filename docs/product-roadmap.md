# EnviroQuant Product Roadmap

# 1. Vision

EnviroQuant is being developed as:

- an AI-powered Environmental Intelligence Platform
- a SaaS platform for EIA creation and evaluation
- a future Data-as-a-Service (DaaS) environmental intelligence layer

The system focuses on:
- structured environmental reasoning
- compliance validation
- traceable findings
- deterministic evaluation workflows

---
